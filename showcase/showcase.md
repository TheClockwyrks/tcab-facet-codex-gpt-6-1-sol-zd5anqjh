# Facet

Every clear leaves a mark. Facet is a puzzle of seven kinds of cut stone, a quiet board, and the beautiful consequences of one well-placed swap. Hold a gem, carry it onto its neighbor, and release to shatter a line of three or more.

The stones left beside a clear take strain. A hairline becomes a fracture; a fracture becomes a flaw. Touch a flawed stone with your next clear and it shatters too, worth double and ready to carry the pressure onward. A worn corner is an opportunity waiting to happen.

Longer lines earn finer cuts. Four stones leave a brilliant that takes the ring around it. Crossing lines leave a star that cuts through a whole row and column. Five or more leave a prism: trade it with a stone to shatter every gem of that kind.

Fresh stones fall into the gaps and can start another clear. Every step climbs the chain multiplier. Reach your target to open the next level, and keep finding moves until the board has nothing left to give.
